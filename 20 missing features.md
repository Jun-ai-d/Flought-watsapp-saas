# 20 High-Impact Features Flought is Missing (Competitor Research)

Based on a deep dive into 5 major competitors in the WhatsApp Business SaaS space (**AiSensy, WATI, Interakt, Zoko, and Gallabox**), I have identified 20 highly impactful features that they offer but Flought currently lacks. 

Flought currently excels at **AI-driven Customer Support**. However, these competitors command massive market share because they act as **Sales and Marketing Engines**. Adding these features to Flought would exponentially increase its value proposition and revenue potential.

Here are the 20 features categorized by their business impact:

---

## 📈 Category 1: Outbound Marketing (Revenue Drivers)
*These features are the bread-and-butter of AiSensy and Gallabox. They turn WhatsApp from a reactive support channel into a proactive sales channel.*

1. **Bulk CSV Broadcasts:** The ability to upload a CSV of 10,000 opted-in contacts and send a promotional template (e.g., "Diwali Sale 50% Off") in one click. 
   * *Benefit:* Direct, immediate revenue generation for the tenant.
2. **Broadcast Scheduling:** Scheduling campaigns to go out at specific, high-conversion times (e.g., Friday at 10:00 AM) without manual intervention.
3. **Click-to-WhatsApp (CTWA) Ads Attribution:** Tracking which specific Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ad generated a WhatsApp conversation, allowing the business to calculate Return on Ad Spend (ROAS).
4. **Drip Campaigns / Sequences:** Sending a timed series of messages automatically (e.g., Day 1: Welcome, Day 2: Product Demo video, Day 3: 10% Discount code).
   * *Benefit:* Automates lead nurturing, vastly increasing conversion rates over time.
5. **Audience Segmentation & Tags:** Grouping users (e.g., "VIP Customers", "Churn Risk", "Bought in Last 30 Days") to send highly targeted broadcasts instead of spamming the entire list.

## 🛒 Category 2: E-Commerce & Sales (Conversion Drivers)
*These features are the core offerings of Zoko and Interakt, designed specifically for Shopify/D2C brands.*

6. **Native Shopify/WooCommerce Catalog Sync:** Connecting the merchant's store so their live product catalog is accessible and browsable natively inside the WhatsApp chat UI.
7. **Automated Abandoned Cart Recovery:** Automatically detecting when a user leaves items in a Shopify cart and sending a WhatsApp message 30 minutes later with a direct checkout link.
   * *Benefit:* This single feature often recovers 15-20% of lost sales and pays for the SaaS subscription itself.
8. **Automated Order & Shipping Updates:** Hooking into fulfillment systems (like Shiprocket) to automatically send "Order Confirmed" and "Out for Delivery" alerts.
9. **In-Chat Native Checkout/Payments:** Allowing the customer to click "Pay Now" via WhatsApp Pay, Razorpay, or Stripe directly inside the chat interface without bouncing to a website.
10. **Cash on Delivery (COD) Confirmation Flows:** Automatically asking the user to click "Confirm Order" via WhatsApp before the business ships a COD item.
    * *Benefit:* Massively reduces RTO (Return to Origin) losses for D2C brands, saving them thousands in shipping costs.

## ⚙️ Category 3: CRM & Workflow Automation (Operational Efficiency)
*These features are where WATI dominates, allowing complex businesses to manage their pipelines.*

11. **Visual Drag-and-Drop Flow Builder:** A no-code canvas that lets tenants build rigid "Press 1 for Sales, Press 2 for Support" decision trees.
    * *Benefit:* While Flought's AI is smarter, many businesses want 100% strict control over the user journey for lead qualification.
12. **Native CRM Integrations:** Out-of-the-box, one-click connections to Salesforce, HubSpot, and Zoho to automatically create "Leads" or "Tickets" when a WhatsApp chat starts.
13. **Zapier / Make (Integromat) Webhooks:** Allowing tenants to connect their Flought bot to 5,000+ other apps (e.g., "When someone fills a Google Form, send a WhatsApp message").
14. **Custom User Attributes (Mini-CRM):** Allowing agents to store structured data against a WhatsApp number right in the dashboard (e.g., Email, Company Name, Lead Score).
15. **Agent SLAs & Auto-Escalation:** Setting rules like "If a human agent doesn't reply in 15 minutes, alert the Manager or auto-reassign the chat."
16. **Internal Agent Notes & @Mentions:** Allowing your human agents to leave hidden notes on a conversation or tag a colleague for help without the customer seeing it.

## 🚀 Category 4: Growth Tools & Infrastructure (Acquisition & UX)
*Features that make it easier for tenants to acquire leads and manage the platform.*

17. **Floating Website Chat Widget:** Generating a simple snippet of code that tenants can paste on their website to display a "Chat with us on WhatsApp" floating button.
    * *Benefit:* The easiest way for a tenant to start getting inbound leads immediately.
18. **QR Code & Pre-filled Link Generator:** A tool to create `wa.me` links with pre-typed messages (e.g., "I'm interested in the Summer Collection") to use in Instagram Bios or print flyers.
19. **Template Creation & Approval UI:** Allowing tenants to design, submit, and track the approval status of Meta message templates directly inside the Flought dashboard, without forcing them to log into the complex Meta Business Manager.
20. **Template Performance Analytics:** Dashboards specifically tracking the Read Rate, Delivery Rate, and Reply Rate of marketing templates to see which campaigns are actually working.

---

### 💡 The "Huge Impact" Conclusion
If Flought builds **Category 1 (Broadcasts) and Category 2 (Abandoned Cart / Shopify Sync)**, you will instantly transition from being just a "Customer Support Tool" to a **"Revenue Generating Machine"**. 

Businesses view support as a *cost center*, but they view marketing/sales tools as an *investment*. Adding these features makes it exponentially easier to sell Flought to clients because you can promise a direct return on investment (ROI).
